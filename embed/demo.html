Budget and council decisions

This is where a city's own page content goes: the official notice, agendas and staff reports. The Municipal Agents card sits beside it and links back to the official notice; it never replaces it.

Add a card to your page

One line of HTML. Change data-decision to the decision's id.

<script src="https://municipal-agents.fly.dev/embed.js"
        data-decision="row-2027-budget"
        data-lang="en"></script>

Several cards with one script:

<div data-municipal-agents data-decision="row-2027-budget"></div>
<div data-municipal-agents data-decision="row-water-supply-2051" data-lang="fr"></div>
<script src="https://municipal-agents.fly.dev/embed.js" defer></script>
data-decisionRequired. The decision id, as in /d/<id>.
data-langen or fr labels; a cached translation of the summary is used when one exists. Defaults to the page's language.
data-themeauto (follows the visitor's setting), light or dark.

Privacy: the card sets no cookies, stores nothing in the browser, sends no referrer and loads one JSON file from municipal-agents.fly.dev. Its styles live in a Shadow DOM, so they don't change your page. Under 15 KB compressed.
