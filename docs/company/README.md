# Municipal Agents: company docs

These documents present Municipal Agents as a B2G vertical-AI company: who buys it,
how, at what price, and why a city's IT, privacy and legal staff can say yes. They were
written on 14 November 2026, at the end of CIVIC Hack 2026.

**Honest status:** a working prototype on public Waterloo Region data, live at
<https://municipal-agents.fly.dev>. We have no customers, pilots, letters of intent or
revenue yet, and we are not affiliated with the Region of Waterloo or its municipalities.
Every number links to a source with a date. Our own arithmetic is marked **estimate**.
