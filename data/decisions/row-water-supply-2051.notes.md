# Water Supply Strategy Update: where the facts are

Pages are 1-based pages of `data/sources/<id>.pages.json`. Budget tables are in $ thousands.

## What is being decided

- The Region is choosing a long-term plan for drinking water to 2051 for the
  Integrated Urban System (Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge and some township communities):
  which new water sources to develop, conservation programs, and an implementation plan
  with high-level costs (`row-water-supply-slides-2024` p7, p12, p14).
- It is a Master Plan under Phases 1 and 2 of the Municipal Class Environmental
  Assessment (`row-water-supply-notice-2` p1; `row-water-supply-slides-2024` p10).
- Now (Nov 2026): Public Meeting No. 2 presents the **shortlisted alternatives** and the
  evaluation approach for picking the preferred one, and asks what matters most to people
  (`row-water-supply-notice-2` p1). The shortlist itself is NOT in our sources yet (the
  slides are posted after 17 Nov). Do not name alternatives.
- Evaluation criteria people were asked about include "Provides low lifecycle costs" and
  "Provides financial sustainability", climate adaptation, protecting groundwater, rivers,
  wildlife and heritage (`row-water-supply-slides-2024` p18).
- About 150 million litres of treated water a day are supplied in the Integrated Urban
  System (`row-water-supply-slides-2024` p17). Region-wide: 125 million litres a day,
  50 treatment facilities, 100+ wells (`row-2026-budget-book` p59). The two figures cover
  different things and dates; do not mix them.

## Costs (cost lens)

- Water Supply 10-year capital program 2026-2035: **$847,572K**; 2027 forecast $73,470K;
  "Upgrades, Expansion & New Facilities" $487,917K over 10 years; funded by water
  development charges $311,904K and the Water Capital Reserve (user rates) $525,407K
  (`row-2026-budget-book` p63). Project lines incl. 4007 Water Supply Master Plan, water
  efficiency programs (p64), 4014 Integrated Urban System Groundwater Supply (p66).
- Water infrastructure renewal funding gap **$23.2M a year** (need $54.7M vs $31.5M
  planned) (`row-infrastructure-gap-2026` p1). Wastewater gap $21.2M (p2).
- Household bills: 2026 water $243.63 and wastewater $301.61 a year for the average
  household, both +5.9% (`row-2026-budget-book` p11).
- No lifecycle cost for any alternative is published yet: an unknown until the shortlist
  and evaluation are released.

## Housing and growth (housing lens)

- Updates are needed to match "Existing and future growth needs" (`row-water-supply-slides-2024` p8).
- The Region reviews about 400 development applications a year for water and wastewater
  (`row-2026-budget-book` p59). The asset management review mentions staff work on the
  "water capacity constraint" (`row-asset-management-review-2026` p4). Water capacity
  limits where and when new homes can connect; no unit figures in these sources.

## Commitments touched

- Guiding principles: minimize climate change risk and energy use, protect sources,
  collaborate, align with the Strategic Plan (`row-water-supply-slides-2024` p9).
- Growth to about one million residents is the Region's planning assumption
  (`grt-business-plan-2025` p2 mentions growing "towards one million residents").
